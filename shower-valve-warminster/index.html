What is behind the wall

The shower valve mixes hot and cold water and sends it to the showerhead or tub spout. The handle you touch is only the trim. The cartridge inside the valve wears out over time, and the valve body itself can fail.

Related work includes shower valve repair, shower cartridge replacement, shower faucet leaks, and mixing valve replacement.

How replacement goes

  1. Describe the problemCall and tell us about the leak, temperature swing, or shutoff issue.
  2. Access the valveTrim removed; wall opened only if there is no rear access.
  3. Replace and connectOld valve or cartridge out, new one fitted to the supply lines.
  4. TestCheck temperature control, shutoff, and leaks before finishing.

Questions

How do I know the valve, not just the trim, is the problem?

Leaks at the handle, or water that will not shut off fully, can come from a worn cartridge or a failing valve body. A plumber can inspect the valve to tell which it is.

Is a repair or a full valve replacement needed?

Often a new cartridge fixes temperature or drip issues. Replacement is considered when the valve body is damaged, corroded, or discontinued. Call to talk through what you are seeing.

Will the wall need to be opened?

Sometimes there is rear access from an adjoining room. Otherwise the wall behind the shower controls may need to be opened to reach the valve.
