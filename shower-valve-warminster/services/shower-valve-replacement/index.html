Shower Valve Replacement in Warminster, PA

Replacement is different from a simple repair. A worn cartridge can often be swapped without replacing the valve. When the valve body is cracked, corroded, or leaking inside the wall, the whole valve is replaced.

Signs to call

  • Dripping shower faucet or handle leak
  • Water will not fully shut off
  • Hot and cold no longer mix properly
  • Handle is stiff or loose

Questions

How do I know the valve, not just the trim, is the problem?

Leaks at the handle, or water that will not shut off fully, can come from a worn cartridge or a failing valve body. A plumber can inspect the valve to tell which it is.

Is a repair or a full valve replacement needed?

Often a new cartridge fixes temperature or drip issues. Replacement is considered when the valve body is damaged, corroded, or discontinued. Call to talk through what you are seeing.

Will the wall need to be opened?

Sometimes there is rear access from an adjoining room. Otherwise the wall behind the shower controls may need to be opened to reach the valve.
